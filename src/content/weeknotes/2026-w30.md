I had a pretty busy and good week! The start of the week was spent down in my
old home town for a little bit with family, and I had my first massage while
there. It was very nice and my muscles are feeling a lot better now as a result
of it. Really need to figure out taking better care of myself and doing
stretches and stuff to be honest, but this was a nice experience to have. While
down there, I also finished reading my first book in years!; a
privately-circulated story given by one of my girlfriends. I really enjoyed it,
and have put together a reading list of a bunch of book suggestions from some
friends that I'm going to go through and see if I like the blurb/general idea
and then try to read them. I forgot how good it was to have my brain stew on the
characters & concepts after reading a book.

I've been in a bit of a
[Half-Life](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half-Life_(video_game)>) mood
recently, with YouTube feeding me a bunch of stuff around it and whatnot. So I
decided to finally play through Black Mesa properly. I last touched it sometime
before they did the full release with Xen some years back, finishing what there
was of the game at the time, so I have been meaning to get back to it. I've
progressed pretty quick through the game, getting up to the first few Xen
levels, and it has been a lot of fun. If you want to experience Half-Life in a
way that isn't the original game, this is definitely the way to do it!

I had a sudden blast of motivation to revisit one of my older projects,
[Prismaliser](https://prismaliser.app), to do some refactoring I've been wanting
to do for ages, having the parsing and formatting work browser-side instead of
having an API do them; moving away from Next.js because I didn't actually need
like any of it at all really, I was just using it as a scaffold; and I decided
on a whim to add [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com) support for it, allowing
public shares to be made and also having bigger schemas shared. The main reason
for this last change was I don't want to have to bother with storing the random
schemas people will share in my own database and having to keep that secure.
This way, people own their data and they know it's public, easier for me to
reason about.

And lastly, I went to the hair salon again! My colour from the last time faded
some time ago so I've been meaning to make another trip and finally got around
to it on Friday. This time I had my hair bleached for the first time ever, so
that the colour would be more vibrant and noticeable, and I also got a sidecut
done because it's a hairstyle that's been on my mind for a while so I figured
I'd give it a try, and I'm seriously loving the outcome of everything! It looks
and feels great and I don't regret it at all!
